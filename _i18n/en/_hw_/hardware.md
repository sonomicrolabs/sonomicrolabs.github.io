# Custom Designed Hardware
SonoMicro's flow measurement hardware is expertly engineered from the ground up for effortless integration and cost-effectiveness. Our non-invasive measurement techniques make us a leading choice for connecting factories to accurate flow data.

- We use ultrasonic sound for flow measurement.
- Coupled with state-of-the-art industrial IoT technology to relay data to and from the cloud.

# Effortless Integration
With SonoMicro, you can enjoy a smooth transition to connected, data-driven flow measurement without the hassle:

- No system downtime required.
- No pressure drop.
- No plumbing alterations or extra fittings needed.
- No electrician necessary.

# Cost-Effective Data Transmission
Our hardware leverages existing cellular infrastructure for data transmission, ensuring you receive accurate, real-time water flow data without incurring additional costs.
