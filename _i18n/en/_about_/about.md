# Our Mission
SonoMicro's mission is to connect factories and industrial sites with accurate, real-time water flow data. Our non-invasive measurement hardware and cloud platform turn water flow into a live data stream that plant managers and engineers can act on.

# Advanced Flow Measurement Service

At SonoMicro, we build non-invasive water flow measurement technology for connected factories. Our sub-metering hardware clips onto existing pipework, streams data over cellular infrastructure, and puts real-time flow measurements directly into the hands of the people running the plant.

Our team of experts has developed state-of-the-art sub-metering hardware and digital solutions that integrate seamlessly, providing accurate, real-time measurements of water flow. The ease of installation eliminates the need for system downtime, plumbing alterations, or electrical work, and leverages existing cellular infrastructure for data transmission at no extra cost.

Our user-friendly software suite allows managers to access, analyze, and visualize water consumption data, offering invaluable insight into how water moves through their facility and where it's being lost.

Our flexible licensing model makes advanced flow measurement hardware and software accessible to plants and facilities of all sizes.
